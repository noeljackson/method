# Example Profile: Research Investigation

This is an unaccepted example, not a live ProjectProfile. Copy the template and
obtain independent acceptance before relying on local authority.

Method version: `0.2.0`

## Sources of truth

1. Primary sources, original data, and direct observations.
2. Reproducible analysis artifacts.
3. Accepted research questions and decision records.
4. Secondary summaries and prior conclusions.

Later publication does not automatically outrank stronger primary evidence.
Conflicting sources remain visible until the method of reconciliation is
recorded.

## Roles

The evaluator and researcher may be one actor during collection. A claim that
drives a high-consequence decision receives an independent review or a second
evidence source. The direction-setter owns changes to the research question.

## Work scales

- Direct: locate or verify one fact from an authoritative source.
- Work item: answer one research question with a cited deliverable.
- Program: a study with dependent questions, data collection, and publication
  gates.

## Context flags

Program is enabled for a persistent multi-workstream study, Experiment for an
explicit controlled comparison against a fixed baseline, and Secrets for
credentials, bearer material, restricted delivery, or possible exposure.
Flags select context and never grant publication or mutation authority.

## Problem taxonomy

- missing evidence;
- conflicting sources;
- ambiguous definition;
- sampling or measurement defect;
- analysis or inference defect;
- scope drift;
- unavailable primary evidence; and
- publication or reproducibility gap.

## Gates

A completed claim has a citation, source-strength label, explicit inference,
and reproducible analysis where applicable. High-consequence conclusions need
independent corroboration or an explicit uncertainty decision.

## Authority

Reading public sources and producing local analysis are authorized. Contacting
participants, changing shared data, publishing externally, or making a
decision on behalf of the direction-setter requires explicit authority.

## Secrets and restricted material

Access credentials and restricted source material remain in their approved
systems. Prompts, notes, citations, and reproducibility artifacts use approved
non-authorizing references and non-sensitive excerpts only. Publication checks include a
disclosure review, and suspected exposure is escalated to the material owner
without repeating it.
